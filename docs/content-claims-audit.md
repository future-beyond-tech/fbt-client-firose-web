## Follow-up evidence required

Before any removed claim is restored, FIROSE management should provide the current product label plus the applicable licence, test report, clinical substantiation, composition/specification, certificate, or registration record. The approved wording must match the evidence scope, product variant, pack size, market, and validity period exactly.
